Use cases

One monitoring core.
Many ways to use it.

Beacon’s audience has broadened as the core product has become more specific: the common need is persistent, independent visibility into real-world Internet quality.

01

Home Internet

See whether the connection is actually healthy—not merely online—and build evidence around recurring service issues.

02

Remote Work

Track the latency, jitter, packet loss, DNS/HTTPS responsiveness, and short disruptions that can freeze calls, VPN sessions, and remote desktops.

03

Gaming & Streaming

Understand latency variation, jitter, loss, and throughput degradation that users perceive as lag, buffering, or a “drop” even when connectivity remains technically up.

04

Business & Hotspots

Observe customer-facing Wi-Fi and Internet quality at restaurants, campgrounds, marinas, rental properties, venues, and other shared-access locations.

05

HOA / Multifamily

Capture intermittent complaints that self-heal before a support call, then distinguish local Wi-Fi behavior from shared network or upstream impairment.

06

ISPs & MSPs

Place independent user-edge Beacons at sites to identify degradation earlier and approach troubleshooting with evidence instead of anecdotes.

07

Remote Properties

Keep a persistent local observer at locations where no one is available to reproduce an intermittent problem on demand.

08

Engineering Teams

Expose the same simple consumer-grade status through progressively deeper views, down to path telemetry, events, throughput, Wi-Fi metrics, and API data.
