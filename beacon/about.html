About Beacon

Born from monitoring.
Focused on experience.

SunPoint Beacon is being developed independently by Sun Point Global LLC, building on decades of enterprise monitoring and observability experience.

A long-running idea

Distributed monitoring from the user’s side.

The concept builds on earlier enterprise approaches that placed lightweight Raspberry Pi devices close to users to verify wireless availability and report back to a central monitoring platform. Beacon modernizes that idea around Internet experience, dual-path measurement, high-resolution incidents, scoring, and eventually cloud management.

One platform

Simple enough for a home. Deep enough for a NOC.

Beacon is designed around a single core monitoring engine rather than separate products for each audience. The presentation changes—from an A–F Beacon Grade to full engineering telemetry—but the underlying measurements remain consistent.

This background describes a general monitoring approach and does not imply endorsement by any current or former employer or other organization.
