One problem. Many users.

The Internet can be “up”
and still feel broken.

01 / SEE IT

Catch short impairments

Beacon is designed to record brief outages, latency spikes, jitter, packet loss, DNS/HTTPS failures, throughput degradation, and power events that can disappear before support investigates.

02 / EXPLAIN IT

Separate Wi-Fi from Internet

Beacon measures wired and wireless paths independently so a local Wi-Fi problem can be distinguished from a router, ISP, upstream, or power problem.

03 / UNDERSTAND IT

Simple first, deep when needed

A Beacon Grade gives anyone an immediate answer. Home, work, gaming, hotspot, ISP/MSP/HOA, and engineering views expose progressively deeper context.
