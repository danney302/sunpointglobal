Use cases / Potential applications

Different locations.
The same visibility gap.

Internet Experience Monitoring is being explored for the situations below. These are intended use cases, not customer deployments or validated product outcomes.

01

HOAs & multifamily properties

When residents report intermittent service, property teams need a clearer view of where and when issues occur.

Development direction: Explore local observation across shared connectivity environments.

02

Managed Wi-Fi & hotspots

A wireless network can appear available while users still struggle to connect or stay connected.

Development direction: Explore measurements from the user’s side of the Wi-Fi connection.

03

Remote properties & locations

Intermittent problems are especially difficult to investigate when no one is on site.

Development direction: Explore a local probe approach that could gather context between visits.

04

ISPs & managed service providers

Support conversations benefit from evidence that helps separate local and upstream connectivity issues.

Development direction: Explore how user-location observations could inform troubleshooting.
