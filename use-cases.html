Use cases

Reliable Internet matters.
Make it measurable.

From one home to a portfolio of properties, see connection quality from the place it is used.

01

Home users

Understand the interruptions behind buffering, slow browsing and unreliable Wi-Fi. Start with a clear grade and open the measurements when you need more context.

02

Remote work

Review response time, jitter, packet loss and connection reliability when calls, VPN sessions or remote desktops become difficult to use.

03

Gaming

Compare Wired and Wi-Fi readings, track delay and connection stability, and use the longer-term preferred-connection badge to choose a consistent setup.

04

Short-term rental hosts

Place a Beacon at each property and review connectivity remotely through Beacon Cloud. Check whether a device is reporting, compare connection quality over time and export evidence when a guest reports unreliable Internet. Monitoring covers Beacon’s connection, not guests’ browsing activity.

05

Businesses and managed properties

Understand Internet and Wi-Fi reliability at offices, shops, shared spaces and managed properties. Beacon+ adds direct AC-power status and battery-backed observation.

06

MSPs, ISPs and IT teams

Collect measurements through a local Hub, organize cloud views by customer and site, and investigate with technical dashboards and SNMP monitoring. Discuss your fleet size and integration requirements with us.
