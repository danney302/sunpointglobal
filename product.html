The problem

A brief outage can leave
a long trail of questions.

By the time someone investigates, the connection may be working again.

Was it Wi-Fi, the local network, power, the ISP, or the wider Internet? The product is being developed around a simple goal: gather useful evidence close to the user so those conversations can begin with better context.

Planned approach / Subject to development and validation

Observe locally.
Build a clearer picture over time.

01 / DESIGN GOAL

A local vantage point

A lightweight local probe concept to observe short Internet interruptions, latency spikes, packet loss, and Wi-Fi degradation at a home, property, or remote location.

02 / DESIGN GOAL

Context for interruptions

Planned measurements to help isolate whether an issue involves Wi-Fi, the local network, power, the ISP, or the wider Internet.

03 / DESIGN GOAL

Evidence when it matters

Local event capture, power-event detection, and battery-backed operation are design goals intended to preserve useful context during interruptions.

Built from monitoring experience

An established perspective.

The concept builds on decades of enterprise monitoring and observability experience and earlier distributed lightweight-probe monitoring approaches used in enterprise environments.
