The problem

A brief outage can leave
a long trail of questions.

By the time someone investigates, the connection may be working again.

Was it Wi-Fi, the local network, the Internet provider, or something beyond it? The product is being developed around a simple goal: gather useful evidence close to the user so those conversations can begin with better context.

Planned approach / Subject to development and validation

Observe locally.
Build a clearer picture over time.

01 / DESIGN GOAL

A local vantage point

A Raspberry Pi-based probe concept to observe connectivity at a home, property, or remote location.

02 / DESIGN GOAL

Context for interruptions

Planned measurements to help distinguish local Wi-Fi or network issues from upstream Internet problems.

03 / DESIGN GOAL

Evidence when it matters

Battery-backed event capture is a design goal, intended to retain useful context during interruptions.
